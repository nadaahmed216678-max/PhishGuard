UNDERSTAND THE THREAT

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack in which criminals impersonate trusted people or organizations to trick users into revealing sensitive information or clicking dangerous links.

!

Urgent Requests

Messages that pressure you to act immediately or threaten account suspension.

↗

Suspicious Links

Unexpected links or web addresses that imitate legitimate websites.

✉

Unknown Senders

Unexpected messages, unusual sender addresses, or requests for private data.

YOUR SECURITY CHECKLIST

Stay One Step Ahead

Build safer habits with a few simple checks.

  • ✓ Verify the sender's email address.
  • ✓ Inspect links before opening them.
  • ✓ Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • ✓ Confirm unexpected requests through official channels.
  • ✓ Report suspicious messages to the appropriate organization.
